# Homepage

Scope: `src/pages/index.astro`, the axerie.com homepage. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: people who know one Axerie tool and want the others. Action: follow a tool's link. Proof: each tool's real screenshot (light and dark).

Chosen: the standard studio page, played straight at a high craft bar (canon). Seed 9459fddf.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet studio page where each tool's real screenshot does the persuading. Refuses the startup hero with gradient blobs, feature-icon grids and slogans.

OWN-WORLD: Cool off-white ground with near-black ink in light mode, graphite in dark. One deep green accent for small labels and hover. Familjen Grotesk display, Hanken Grotesk text. Screenshots in soft-shadowed 14px frames; pill buttons in ink.

STORY: Who makes this and why it's free, then one band per tool: what it does in plain words, where it runs, one action, its screenshot.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark and GitHub link, a large balanced headline and one-sentence intro, with the first tool's screenshot starting in view at desktop widths so the page reads as tools, not a manifesto.

SIGNATURE: Screenshots follow the visitor's color scheme, and each band alternates sides on wide screens and stacks on phones.

REFUSALS: No eyebrows, no section numbers, no stats, no cards of icons, no bio or photo.
